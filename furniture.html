Established in 2017, Green Nest — a unit of Sri Vasavi Interiors and Exteriors — has become a trusted name in modular home and office furniture manufacturing in Bengaluru.

We specialise in customised product development with complete customer satisfaction, using ISO-certified eco-friendly materials and state-of-the-art quality measures.

Our products combine functional design with environmental responsibility — every piece is manufactured using ZED (Zero Defect Zero Effect) technology, ensuring energy-efficient production that meets global standards.
