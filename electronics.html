Venus World

Our
38-Year Legacy

Located in the heart of Malleswaram, Venus World has been serving the people of Bengaluru for over 38 years with an unwavering focus on customer satisfaction, competitive pricing, quality service, and swift delivery.

Venus World is owned by one of the partners of Sri Vasavi Interiors and Exteriors, making it a trusted part of the SVIE family. Together, we offer a truly comprehensive solution for your home and office needs.

We serve customers across the Karnataka region, with two showroom locations in Malleswaram — the second near Sai Baba Temple, specialising in Duroflex mattresses and bedding solutions.
