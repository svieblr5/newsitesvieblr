Who We Are

Sri Vasavi Interiors and Exteriors (SVIE) was founded in 2017 with a simple but powerful mission: to create extraordinary spaces that combine aesthetic excellence with functional precision.

Based in the heart of Malleshwaram, Bengaluru, we serve residential and commercial clients across Karnataka, delivering end-to-end solutions from concept to completion. We use cutting-edge CNC machinery, eco-friendly materials, and ISO-certified processes to guarantee results that exceed expectations.

Our Mission
To prepare the best projects with integrity, craftsmanship, and quality, while contributing economically viable concepts to both commercial and residential projects.
Our Vision
To be among the best and most competitive interiors companies in India — recognized for our innovative design philosophy and commitment to sustainable practices.
Quality Policy
We commit to delivering high-quality products and managing projects excellently, meeting all customer requirements with third-party quality assurance through organizations like QCI.
