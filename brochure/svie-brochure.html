The Company

Who We Are

Since 2017 · Bengaluru

Sri Vasavi Interiors and Exteriors is a Bengaluru-based team of specialists across design, construction, technology and supply. Since 2017 we have brought an innovative format of construction and interiors to clients at competitive prices — growing from a small, ground-up studio into a complete, single-source solutions provider.

Workplaces and living spaces have changed dramatically. We partner with our clients to create new, innovative environments that meet their needs — using environmentally friendly materials and practices, and the discipline of a well-run firm. We are proud of our team and look forward to a long and exciting future, growing together with the people we serve.

Certified Quality Standards

Quality Is Our
Quiet Obsession

Every element of a project — soil compaction, concrete vigour, welding and more — is checked or tested to recognised standards. Quality is managed through two disciplines working together: Quality Control (QC), defining how requirements are met, and Quality Assurance (QA), defining the steps we take to verify them. Reviews continue right up to final completion, so what we hand over genuinely meets the standard set out in the project documents.

Mission

To prepare the best projects with integrity, craftsmanship and quality — contributing economically viable concepts to both commercial and residential work.

Vision

To be among the best and most competitive interiors companies in India, recognised for innovative design philosophy and a commitment to sustainable practices.

Quality Policy

To deliver high-quality products and manage projects excellently, meeting every customer requirement — with third-party quality assurance through QCI.

Environment & Safety

Eco-friendly products and practices, sound health-and-safety standards, and total quality management driven by genuine customer satisfaction.
